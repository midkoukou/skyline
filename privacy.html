Privacy Policy

Skyline Live Weather Wallpaper · Last updated: November 2026

In short: Skyline doesn't collect, store, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your location

If you allow it, Skyline uses your approximate location to show the weather where you are. Your location is used on your Mac only to request the weather from Apple's WeatherKit service and to look up your city's name with Apple's geocoding service. It is never sent to us or to any other company. You can instead type a city in Settings and keep location turned off.

Weather data

Weather is provided by Apple Weather. Apple's handling of these requests is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in your App Store account settings.

Settings stored on your Mac

Your preferences (such as a chosen city, quality and display options) are stored only on your Mac. Skyline's screensaver keeps a copy of the latest weather on your Mac so it can show it while you're away.

Children

Skyline doesn't collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
